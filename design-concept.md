# Design concept — Островок радости Дизарди

## Client / project

- Name: Островок радости Дизарди
- Date: 2026-11-08
- Niche: Children's entertainment center in Mineralnye Vody
- Concept-ID: 2026-11-08-dizardi-paper-island-v1
- Composition-archetype: map-place-led
- Forbidden-for-this-niche: Generic rounded SaaS cards, luxury editorial styling, stock-photo children posed as clients, invented venue features or prices
- Delta-from-previous: n/a — first concept

## Niche fingerprint

- Children arrive with a parent and need a quick way to picture the visit and its cost.
- The center occupies two floors of ТЦ Мар; finding the right entrance and floors is part of the visit.
- Children move between trampolines, slides, an interactive room and broader play areas.
- Birthdays combine an animator, shows, workshops and a cafe/kitchen.
- The everyday price vocabulary is short and practical: hour, two hours, unlimited.
- The place is an indoor family outing with colorful play, movement and parent logistics—not a quiet boutique.

## Unique thesis

1. Mood: a cheerful paper adventure map of a real local play center, drawn with the looseness of a kid's notebook.
2. Type rationale: a sturdy, friendly rounded display face for wayfinding paired with a clean sans for prices, hours and address.
3. Palette logic (from materials/light): sunny yellow paper, cobalt marker ink, watermelon red and pool blue, grounded by warm white.
4. Composition: a map-led, hand-inked island connects the floors and activities; practical visit facts stay in a readable route ledger.
5. Motion: small, slow marker-like reveals; no movement required to understand or use the site.
6. Main gesture: an illustrated island map with numbered, fact-checked pins; secondary: ticket-stub prices and imperfect doodle accents.

## Anti-clone checklist

- [x] Carryover: first project in this workspace; no prior palette/type/layout to reuse.
- [x] Benchmark clone: no reference layout or identity is copied; the local map is the organizing device.
- [x] AI clusters: avoids purple gradients, repeated identical cards, generic dashboard/SaaS patterns and luxury defaults.
- [x] Brand test: even without a logo, the two-floor kids' entertainment map, activity labels and family visit details identify this niche.

## Palette + type

- Palette: sunny paper `#FFE16A` and warm white `#FFF9E9` for a cheerful indoor play setting; marker cobalt `#2458D3`; slide red `#F06A5B`; pool blue `#65C8D5`; leafy green `#64A66D`; dark ink `#253332` for readable contrast.
- Type: Nunito for round, welcoming headlines; Manrope for compact and legible practical details. System fallbacks keep the page usable if web fonts are unavailable.
